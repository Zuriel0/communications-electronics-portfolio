# Nodalume

## Platform

Web. This independent React microsite describes a product under development; it is not its administrative application.

## Confirmed product truth

Nodalume explores captive portals through visual composition, properties, responsive views and a visual logic workspace. The existing application has implementation evidence for these surfaces. Their presence does not establish a complete general-purpose logic engine, external actions, production readiness or commercial deployment.

## Audience and job

This surface introduces the product's creative direction to people evaluating how a portal's identity and connected experiences could be composed. Detailed buyer positioning remains open; no conversion, pricing or adoption claims are inferred.

## Constraints

Keep the administrative frontend, authentication, backend, RADIUS, database, infrastructure and demo projects untouched. No public release, DNS, recordings or production deployment. No private media, credentials, internal endpoints or console URLs. EN/ES from the first version. Distinguish present implementation, partial implementation, concepts and future vision.

## Brand commitments

Creative Systems; warm white, black, electric violet, sparse lime; monumental editorial typography, asymmetry and meaningful motion. Preserve the actual Nodalume mark geometry. Identity deliberately separate from ANXZU.

## Stack

React is specified in the brief. Independent Vite package, GSAP and ScrollTrigger; native scrolling. No new animation framework, auth integration or backend connection.

## Success

A functional local landing with honest content, cinematic motion, static reduced-motion composition, responsive evidence and documented design decisions. Public media and conversion strategy remain future decisions.

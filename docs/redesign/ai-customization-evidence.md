## Requested artifacts for deeper storytelling

- An authorized sanitized before/after example with original constraints and actual customization source/revision.
- The technical-context artifact and generated proposal, plus the human corrections and owner contribution boundaries.
- Validation criteria and real executed observations proving what was preserved and what changed. Distinguish ready-to-integrate from a released change.
- A timing worksheet that defines whether analysis, generation, review, fixes and integration are included; record comparable projects, dates, durations and sample size.
- A concrete lesson grounded in an actual rejected/refined proposal, rather than a hypothetical retrospective.

Until those exist, preserve the public qualification “self-reported comparison; not a controlled benchmark”. Do not derive a certified percentage, guarantee, named client, language, injection technique or autonomous AI capability. Do not confuse portfolio lint/build tests with tests of the original customizations.
